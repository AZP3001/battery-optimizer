Connect your phone

Make your Android battery last much longer. No app on the phone, no root.

  1. On the phone: Settings > About phone > tap Build number 7 times.
  2. Settings > Developer options > turn on USB debugging.
  3. Plug it in, press Connect, pick the phone, then tap Allow on the phone.

Needs Chrome or Edge on a computer. Close other ADB programs first (adb kill-server). On Windows the phone may need its USB driver.
This website is experimental. The scripts are recommended for now.
