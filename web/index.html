Battery Optimizer

Make your Android phone's battery last much longer. Runs in your browser over a USB cable, with no app on the phone and no root.

1. Connect your phone

  1. On the phone: Settings > About phone > tap Build number 7 times.
  2. Settings > Developer options > turn on USB debugging.
  3. Plug it in, press Connect, pick the phone, then tap Allow on the phone.

Needs Chrome or Edge on a computer (WebUSB). If another ADB program is running, close it first (adb kill-server). On Windows the phone may need its USB driver.

Phone

Optimized apps cannot run in the background, hold wake locks or start foreground services. Green = fully optimized. Yellow = system part, not recommended.
